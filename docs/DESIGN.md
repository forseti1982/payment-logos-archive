# Design rules

## Master assets
Master artwork is kept separate from wallee presentation. Do not add borders, shadows, rounded rectangles or wallee colors to a brand-owner master asset.

## Standard tile
The generated tile provides a consistent visual footprint:
- fixed aspect ratio and dimensions defined by the generator
- 1 px white outer edge
- 2 px wallee turquoise `#11D9CC` frame
- white internal logo field
- subtle corner radius
- official mark centered optically and geometrically without distortion

Brand-owner clearspace/minimum-size rules override any desire to make a mark visually larger.

## Quality gate
A brand cannot become `verified` until:
- first-party source is recorded
- current logo/version is checked
- colors and geometry are checked
- intended acceptance/merchant usage is checked
- required clearspace is recorded
- rendered tile passes visual review
