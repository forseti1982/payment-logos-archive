# Brand audit

## Gate

No payment mark becomes `verified` until both **identity/artwork** and **intended usage** are supported by first-party evidence.

## Evidence priority

1. official brand/scheme/payment-network developer or brand portal
2. official partner/media asset portal
3. official merchant/technical acceptance documentation
4. official brand-guideline document

Third-party logo repositories are discovery aids only.

## Review sequence

### 1 · Product identity
Determine the exact product. Do not conflate corporate brands, payment products, schemes and wallets.

### 2 · Lifecycle
Record current, renamed, legacy, retired or region/device/card-dependent status.

### 3 · Artwork
Check symbol, wordmark, capitalization, geometry, color, marks, approved variants and background rules.

### 4 · Acceptance usage
Confirm whether the mark is permitted/appropriate for terminal UI, checkout, merchant signage or another intended surface. Corporate-logo availability alone is insufficient.

### 5 · Provenance
Record first-party source, checked date, version where available, constraints and audit notes.

### 6 · Rendering
Generate the normalized output and wallee tile.

### 7 · QA
Run automated validation and human visual inspection at realistic UI/terminal sizes.

## Swiss focus

Explicitly audit ep2, PostFinance / PostFinance Card / PostFinance Pay, TWINT, international schemes and materially relevant mobile/wearable wallets.

## Decision rule

When evidence is ambiguous or conflicting, keep the asset at `review`. Never resolve uncertainty by visual guesswork.
